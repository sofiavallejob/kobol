Recording (real synth) Model

Recordings: Álvaro Crespo's Kobol dataset (UPF, 2024), 4 notes × 8 cutoff × 8 resonance. Model: four OTA stages plus the CA3080 resonance limiter from the schematic, fed by the VCO sound rebuilt from the data. For each knob position, the knob-to-filter law was retrained without that position, so every sound here is a prediction. From resonance 111 the filter whistles by itself, which is the self-oscillation.
